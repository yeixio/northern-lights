# Code of conduct

Northern Lights is built for families and small communities, and we want
working on it to feel the same way: welcoming, patient and kind. This code of
conduct is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make taking part in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What we expect

- Being kind and assuming good intent
- Respecting different opinions, experiences and skill levels
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Owning our mistakes, apologizing to those affected, and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the people who use Northern Lights

## What we don't accept

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing other people's private information, such as a home or email
  address, without their explicit permission
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, and
anywhere else you represent the project) and to everyone taking part,
maintainers included.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior concerns you, report it privately to the maintainers
through the repository's
[private reporting form](https://github.com/yeixio/northern-lights/security/advisories/new)
(it's meant for security issues, but it's private and works for conduct reports
too; say that's what it is). Every report will be reviewed promptly and fairly,
and the privacy of the person reporting will be respected.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other
contributions that break this code of conduct, and may respond with, in order
of severity: a private warning, a public warning, a temporary ban from the
project's spaces, or a permanent ban. The response will match the impact of the
behavior, and the person involved will be told why.
