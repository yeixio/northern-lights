Northern Lights app privacy

Last updated 24 October 2026.

What the app is

The Northern Lights app shows the Northern Lights sites you belong to in one place. Each site is run by its own owner, a family, club or group, on their own server. The app is made by yeix.

What we collect

Nothing. The app talks only to the sites you add, straight from your phone. yeix runs no server behind the app, and the app has no analytics, advertising or tracking.

What stays on your phone

The list of sites you added, a sign-in for each one, and a copy of the most recent posts so the app opens quickly. Removing a site, or the app, deletes them. Each sign-in can also be removed from the site, under Get the app, Your devices.

What the sites see

What you post, send and like goes to that site, and its owner looks after it under that site's own rules. A site sees your phone's name ("Northern Lights app on iPhone") and when the app last used its sign-in. Ask the site's owner about anything you've shared there, or to delete your account.

Photos and camera

The app asks for your photos or camera only when you choose to add a photo or video to a post, and sends it only to the site you post on.
